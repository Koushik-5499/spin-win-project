Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 | Last Updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Spin & Win Promotional Platform ("we", "our", or "the Platform") collects, uses, handles, and safeguards your information when you access or interact with our promotional wheel gaming interface.

1. Core Principles: Voluntariness & Granular Consent

We believe in complete privacy transparency. Participation in the promotional wheel is open to all visitors. Granting any device permission (Location, Camera, or Storage) is completely optional and strictly independent. Your prize spin eligibility and promotional outcome are never conditioned upon granting unrelated permissions.

2. Detailed Permission Information

A. Location Data

  • Purpose: Geolocation is requested solely to verify regional eligibility for localized promotional campaigns and to deter fraudulent automated botting.
  • Consent: Location coordinates are obtained only if you explicitly click the "Allow Location" button on the dedicated permissions screen.
  • Data Collected: Approximate Latitude and Longitude coordinates.
  • Control: You may freely click "Skip" without any penalty to your spin or reward.

B. Camera Access

  • Purpose: An optional celebratory winner selfie to verify human participation.
  • Consent & Streaming: The camera stream activates only upon clicking "Start Camera". You can toggle between front and rear cameras.
  • Zero Background Capture: We never record or capture photos in the background. A photo is only created when you deliberately click "Capture Photo".
  • Review & Deletion: You have the option to retake or delete the snapshot before choosing whether to save it. Closing the camera screen stops all hardware streams immediately.

C. Gallery & File Picker

  • Purpose: Uploading optional proof or verification documents at your choice.
  • Mechanism: We use the standard browser file selector. We never scan, index, read, or copy your device gallery.
  • Control: Only individual files deliberately picked and confirmed by you are transmitted.

D. UPI Virtual Payment Address

  • Purpose: Registering your promotional reward claim for administrative review.
  • Important Safeguard: We never request your UPI PIN, OTP, bank login credentials, debit card numbers, or authorization to debit funds.
  • Disclaimer: Submitting a UPI ID registers a claim request; it does not represent an instant or guaranteed banking wire transfer.

3. Data Storage & Cloud Security

Collected information is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections directly to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage. Data is protected by strict server-side Firestore Security Rules that prohibit unauthorized visitors from reading or modifying records belonging to other participants.

4. Consent Withdrawal

You hold the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Clicking "Withdraw All Consents" on the permissions screen logs your withdrawal in our Firestore consent audit log and updates your participant profile immediately.

5. Non-Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal information, location coordinates, or captured images to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

6. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding your participant data, consent logs, or to request permanent record deletion, contact our privacy officer at: privacy@spinwin-project.local.
